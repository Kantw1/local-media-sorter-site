LOCAL MEDIA SORTER

A little space for what matters.

A Windows desktop app for reviewing local photos and videos, setting unwanted files aside safely, finding exact duplicates, and exporting resized or organized copies.

Support

Contact scony2200@gmail.com. Include your app version and a description of the issue. Avoid sending private photos, passwords, or sensitive file paths.

Your files stay in your control

Keep leaves a file in place. Quarantine moves it to a separate folder. Undo can restore it when the original destination is available. Review quarantine before deleting anything yourself.
