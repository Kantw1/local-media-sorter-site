Local photos and videos

Local Media Sorter reads files in folders you select. Keep leaves files in place. Quarantine moves files into a separate folder while preserving relative folder structure. The app does not upload your media or permanently delete it. Duplicate detection compares file contents locally. Resize and organization tools create local copies. No external AI service processes your files.

Information stored on your computer

Sorting progress, file paths, recent actions, and move-recovery records are stored in your Windows user profile. These records support Resume, Undo, and recovery after interruption. They may contain file and folder names. Language, appearance, and workspace preferences are also stored locally. Other software or Windows backup settings may sync selected folders independently of this app.

Advertising and purchases

The currently released version 1.2.0 contains no advertising SDK, application analytics, cloud account connections, or active payment flow. It does not require an app account. Store licensing integration is in development. Microsoft processes Store account information, payments, and licensing under its own policies when Microsoft Store services are used. The publisher does not receive your payment-card details.

Advertising is planned but is not active. Before enabling an advertising provider, this policy will be updated to identify that provider, describe the information it receives and its purposes, and explain the available consent and privacy controls. Media, previews, filenames, and folder paths will not be shared with advertisers.

Your controls

You choose the source and quarantine folders. Review quarantine before deleting files yourself. Starting a fresh session clears the active sorting session; move-recovery records may remain. Uninstalling the app does not intentionally remove source or quarantine media. To remove local sorting metadata, you may remove the app’s profile records after completing any necessary recovery or Undo.

Support and updates

If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond to your request. Do not send private media or passwords. Contact the address above for questions or requests concerning support correspondence. This policy will be updated when the app’s data handling changes.
